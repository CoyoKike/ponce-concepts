Safe timetogether.

A neutral, safe place for children to keep seeing their parents while a custody case is in court.

Start registration

Children should not carry the weight of a custody case.

So we give them safe, positive time with the people they love, and leave the arguments to the court. We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and a neutral third party.

Visit:

At the park

Visits at parks, restaurants and other child-friendly places, with two supervisors on every outing. $90 / hour

With a coach

Interactive supervision: live feedback from a mental health professional or trained supervisor. Not therapy. $100 / hour

At home

Supervised visits in the home, so routines and familiar things stay in place. $120 / hour
